Mobile Devices Simulators

Every hands-on simulator in the course, in Learning Plan order. Each one runs in your browser, costs nothing and saves your progress on the device you use. Finish its checklist, then copy the report with its verification code into your Solve It.

Use the same browser and device each time. Progress is saved in your browser, not your Blackboard account. Clearing your browser data or switching computers starts a simulator over.
